## 2. Verification readiness: **Moderate**

**Profile type:** a service-area business with the address hidden.
- Google: "If you don't serve customers at your business address, remove your address". You may list up to 20 service areas, within about 2 hours' drive of the base.
- Primary category: **Personal trainer**.
- Name: the real registered brand name only, with no "Chicago" or "in-home" stuffing.

**Why Moderate rather than Easy:**
- No license is needed (NASM: no US state licenses trainers).
- But a service-area business has no storefront sign. Google's video asks for "business name clearly printed on a permanent fixture", so proof has to come from a branded vehicle, apparel and documents that match the profile name.

### What makes Google reject it, and how to avoid that

| Rejection cause | Avoid it by |
|---|---|

**Offer:** $45 per session delivered.
- That's the top of Svetness's range and matches Mode Body's ceiling.
- Add a guaranteed weekly minimum once the trainer has 10 clients (**Assumption**).

**Required:** an NCCA-accredited CPT (NASM, ACE, ACSM or NSCA), plus CPR/AED. Insurers usually require the accredited cert.

**Worker-classification caution: don't 1099 trainers who work under our brand.**
- Illinois presumes employment for unemployment insurance unless all three prongs of 820 ILCS 405/212 are proven: (A) free from control; (B) outside the usual course of business, or outside all places of business; (C) an independently established business.
- In-home work may meet B. But A and C fail when 6 Central sets the schedule, rates and brand.
- As W-2 employer we owe:
  - **Workers' comp:** required from the first part-time hire.
  - **Chicago paid leave:** 1 hr paid leave plus 1 hr paid sick leave per 35 hrs worked, each capped at 40 hrs/yr.
  - **FLSA travel time:** travel between client homes during the workday is hours worked (29 CFR 785.38). Per-session pay must still clear the Chicago minimum wage across all hours, travel included.

- Tommy Else: $90-125. JMc: from $90/hr.

**Our price:**
- 12-week programs: **$95/session at 2×/wk ($2,280)** and $90 at 3×/wk.

**Capacity per trainer (Assumption: 20 sessions/week part-time):**
- About 87 sessions/month, or 10 clients at 2×/wk.
- That's about $8,230 revenue, $3,390 gross margin, and **$3,170/month contribution** before CAC and owner time.

**Illinois contract law: the Physical Fitness Services Act (815 ILCS 645) applies.** It covers "any person… offering physical fitness services to the public", and the definition includes personal training. Every package needs:
- a written contract, with a copy given at signing, and originals kept for the term plus 3 years
- the customer's total payment obligation
- cancellation with a full refund within 3 business days
- a pro-rated exit for death or disability
- an exit for moving more than 25 miles away (fee capped at the lesser of 10% of the unused balance or $50)
- refunds within 30 days
- an initial term of 1 year or less

A non-compliant contract is void, and damages can be tripled (§§ 9, 11). Auto-renewals must also follow the Automatic Contract Renewal Act.

**Not confirmed:** whether a bond applies. None was found in §§ 2-12 as reviewed; have the attorney confirm.

**Google platforms:**
- **Local Services Ads now appear on "in home personal trainer" in Chicago** (seen 2026-10-04 15:47 UTC; absent at 15:28).
- Personal trainer is an LSA category. It's Google Screened, with a background check and a headshot.

- AI Overviews appear on the "personal trainer" Chicago results. People Also Ask shows "How much does it cost to have a personal trainer come to my house?" A pricing page with real numbers is the asset AI answers can cite.

**Regulators:**
- The Illinois Attorney General enforces the Physical Fitness Services Act (treble damages).
- Chicago BACP enforces licensing.
- Nutrition: give general, non-medical guidance only, and never call anyone a "dietitian" or "nutritionist". The Illinois Dietitian Nutritionist Practice Act protects those titles.

## 8. Risks and kill criteria

| # | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | We can't hire or keep a certified trainer at $45/session. Experienced Evanston roles pay $60-85/hr; Svetness pays up to $45/hr. | Offer a weekly minimum and zoned routes that cut unpaid gaps. Keep 3 candidates warm. Fall back to Partner & manage. |
| 2 | Worker misclassification. | W-2 only, with workers' comp from day one, Chicago paid leave, and travel time counted. Use 1099 only for true independents with their own brand, clients and insurance. |
| 3 | Verification fails or the profile is suspended. | A real base, a real name, documents that match the name, the one-take script. Never a virtual office or keyword name. |
| 4 | Local demand is thinner than the national trend. "in home personal trainer chicago" is 20/mo; neighborhood terms are 30-40/mo. | Rank the generic "personal trainer" near the base. Add LSA and referrals. Hold paid spend to the CAC ceiling. |
| 5 | Travel time, cancellations and contract non-compliance eat the margin. | One neighborhood per trainer per half-day. A late-cancel clause. 4-week billing. An attorney-reviewed contract under the Physical Fitness Services Act. |
