# Mobile marine services, Chicagoland: operator plan

**Slug:** `mobile-marine-services-chicagoland` · **Written:** 2026-10-04 · **Portfolio:** Home-services project (config cluster `auto-at-home` → `mobile marine`)

**What it is:** a mobile crew that shrink-wraps boats in the fall, unwraps and recycles the film in spring, details boats in summer, and later winterizes and de-winterizes them. It covers Lake County IL (the Chain O'Lakes: Fox Lake, Antioch), Waukegan/Winthrop Harbor and the Chicago lakefront.

**Data used:** the 6 Central niche study (OpenRush/Semrush, 2026-10-04), plus new pulls made for this plan: three 24-month OpenRush trends, three new map packs and one Semrush batch. Raw files are in `runs/video-niches/serp/` and `runs/video-niches/keywords/`. The plan used 21 OpenRush credits and 80 Semrush units.

---

## 1. Verdict in one line

**Go at a set date.** The full launch is **March 1, 2027** (spring unwrap, recycling and detailing), and the main money season is **fall 2027**. Fall 2026 happens only as a **capped shrink-wrap-only pilot (15 boats, home-stored boats on the Chain)**, and only if a trained installer and boat-in-our-care insurance are both in place by **Fri Oct 16, 2026**.

**The reason:** demand is not the problem. More than half of the year's searches are still ahead: Oct-Dec carried 57% of 2025's "boat winterization near me" searches. The problem is that nobody on the team can wrap or winterize yet. Training a new crew on customers' boats at the peak is the fastest way to a bad first review and a damage claim.

## 2. Verification readiness: **Moderate**

It becomes Easy once the equipment, the vehicle branding, the insurance certificate and the first invoice exist. Today none of them do.

- **Profile type:** service-area business with the address hidden. Google: "If you're a service-area business, you should hide your business address from customers." The service area must stay within "about 2 hours of driving time from where your business is based", with up to 20 areas.
- **Base:** the real place the crew starts from and the equipment is stored. That is either 6 Central's Chicago base, or the lead installer's Lake County home or garage if the equipment actually lives there.
  - A base in Lake County ranks better on the Chain.
  - Never use a marina, a mailbox or a co-working desk. Google says a virtual office "isn't eligible".
- **Services on the profile at launch:** shrink wrap, shrink-wrap removal and recycling, and boat detailing (from spring).
  - **Do not list winterization** until a qualified technician on our own payroll does the work (target: fall 2027). Listing a service we refer out misrepresents the business.

## 7. Two and three steps ahead

**Right after launch**
- **The first big snow or wind event (Dec-Feb)** tests every wrap while the crew is off-season. Budget a free re-tape callback, and keep a winter repair kit.
- **Spring removal produces about 20 lb of film per boat.** The pilot recovered ~26,000 lb a year from ~1,300 boats at Liberty Marina, Munson Marine, SkipperBud's and Montrose Harbor; ~20 lb is computed from those figures.
  - Offer recycling through the Dr. Shrink Recycling Run ($5 EZ Fill bags; Illinois eligible).
  - This is a true differentiator, and it matches the mission.

**How others react**
- **Marinas** (Fox Lake Harbor 170 reviews, SkipperBud's 163, Larsen) sell storage and wrap together. Expect closed yards. The answer is to stay home-storage-first and offer to be a marina's overflow vendor.
- **Unlimited Shrinkwrap** holds #1 on relevance alone and may chase reviews once a rival appears. Mr. Jamber (Michigan, 63 reviews) holds Chicago.
- **Mobile Boat Repair** could add wrapping. A referral partnership reduces that risk.
- **Google:**
  - Verification is by video, and suspensions hit names and addresses that break the rules.
  - **AI Overviews** already sit on "boat shrink wrap" (Chicago) and "boat shrink wrap near me". Publish a plain per-foot price page so "how much does it cost..." answers cite us.
  - No Local Services Ads appear in any pack checked.
  - Fox Lake's organic results for "boat shrink wrap" are all YouTube and Facebook. A short video of each wrap, filmed with permission, can rank there.
- **Regulators:**
  - No state license found.
  - The Park District requires vendor approval.
  - Check whether the village where the base sits requires a home-business license.
  - Detailing at marinas must follow Illinois Clean Marina practices on wash water.
- **The labor market:** marinas hire technicians in September (Bob's Marina posted Sep 11). Hire early.

**Build now so nothing is rebuilt later**
- An owned domain with a price page.
- A tracked phone number separate from TJ's.
- A CRM with a boat record per customer: length, beam, engine, where it's stored, wrap date.
- A review request sent after every job.
- Written SOPs: wrap checklist, photo log, storm callback, recycling, and a signed winterization checklist. Insurers deny freeze claims without proof of proper winterization.
- Insurance limits that already meet the Chicago Harbors vendor spec.

**Next services, and how this feeds the portfolio**
- **Order of adding services:** detailing (Fox Lake detailing pack median 42), then in-house winterization (fall 2027).
- **TJ's Nationwide Roadside** shares dispatch and the answering line.
- **The car-detailing project** shares detailing gear and the summer crew.
- **Home-services clusters** (snow removal, holiday lighting) give the seasonal crew winter work, which keeps them through the year.
- **Lake-home owners** are the same buyers for gutter cleaning and pressure washing.
- **Portfolio flag:** the tracked listing "TJ 24 Hour Mobile Tire Repair Service Chicago IL" carries service and location words. Google's rules bar both unless that is the business's real, consistently used name. Confirm it, so a policy sweep doesn't reach the whole account.

## 8. Risks and kill criteria

| # | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A heat tool burns, scorches gelcoat or starts a fire on a customer's boat | Care-custody-control insurance; a fire extinguisher at every job; tape fuel vents and pad corners; photo log before and after; trained lead only. |
| 2 | Freeze damage from a winterization we did. Insurers exclude freeze damage without proof of proper winterization; one operator's three-engine claim exceeded $85,000 (Aviva). | No engine work until an in-house certified technician is hired; partner by referral until then; signed checklist and receipt per job. |
| 3 | No installer or technician at peak, because marinas hire the same people the same month | Recruit in Jan-Feb and by July; pay at the top of the $20-30 range; give winter work through the other portfolio projects. |
| 4 | Marina access is closed (the Park District allows approved vendors only; private-yard rules are unpublished) | Start with home-stored boats; apply for Park District approval for 2027; ask the Waukegan and North Point operators before marketing there. |
| 5 | A short or shrinking season: early freeze, or the 16-19% year-over-year search dip | Pre-book with deposits; keep fixed costs to insurance plus one vehicle; detailing carries the spring and summer. |

**Kill criteria.** Day 0 is the first paid wrap in the pilot. With no pilot, Day 0 is Mar 1, 2027.

| Checkpoint | On track | Stop or partner out if |
|---|---|---|
| Day 30 | ≥8 paid jobs; 0 damage incidents; profile verified | <4 paid jobs, **any** fire or hull-damage incident, or verification rejected twice |
| Day 60 | ≥15 jobs; ≥8 Google reviews averaging ≥4.7; contribution ≥30% per job; ≥10 deposits for spring or fall | Contribution <20% after acquisition cost, or <5 reviews |
| Day 90 | Wrap callback rate ≤10%; in the top 3 for "boat shrink wrap" in the Fox Lake or Antioch pack (track in the daily scout); lead installer committed for next season | Not in the top 3 **and** <10 deposits. Then refer wrap leads to an established operator and keep only detailing. |
