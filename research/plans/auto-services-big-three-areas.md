**Licensing:** the Illinois Locksmith Act requires a license to hold out as a locksmith. Older legislation
exempted towing-company employees opening vehicles under set conditions; confirm that against the current
225 ILCS 447 with IDFPR. The listing must belong to the licensed (or exempt) operator.
