What we kept from the video, and what we dropped

  • Kept: look where other marketers don’t, favor big-ticket jobs, trust search intent, research before spending, line up several real operators per city, sell repeat bookings.
  • Dropped: ranking a Google profile for a business you don’t run and forwarding the calls. Google bars lead-generation businesses from holding profiles.
  • Dropped: “celebrity” wording without celebrity clients, and listings for institutions (airports, passport offices, credit unions).
  • Unverified claims: “instant rankings”, “no competition in every city”, “$9,000 per deal”.
