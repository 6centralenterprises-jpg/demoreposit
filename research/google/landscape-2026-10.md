# Google Local and GBP Landscape, as of 2026-10-04

For 6 Central Enterprises (Chicago). [S#] cites the sources listed at the end. "Tier C" marks agency blogs.

## 1. Suspension and re-verification waves in 2026

- **Confirmed by trade press:** in early May 2026, suspensions spiked because Google restricted the accounts managing the profiles. Many accounts were hit within 24 hours. The cause is unknown and Google made no statement [S1].
- **Agency-reported only (Tier C):**
  - Apr 27: a "Deceptive Content" mass suspension, mostly in California. Garage door was hit hardest, then locksmith, landscaping and contractors [S2].
  - May 3–4: account restrictions hit home services and accounts running LSA or Ads [S3].
  - Garage door was hit across North America, blamed on tangled agency and Ads/LSA account setups [S4].
  - HVAC, pest, roofing, plumbing and junk removal were also hit, with keyword names and virtual or home addresses as triggers [S5].
- Newly claimed profiles get suspended after bursts of edits [S6], following Google's Apr 16 Gemini edit moderation [S7].
- **Correction:** the often-cited garage door/dumpster/personal-injury suspension wave was Sept 2024, not 2026 [S8].
- Reinstated profiles increasingly need re-verification [S9]. Appeals have accepted evidence uploads since July [S10].
- No 2026 wave was found for towing, water damage, movers, appliance repair, cleaning, legal or medical.

## 2. Verification changes

- **Feb 2026:** the verification flow now asks for the business model. A wrong pick can trigger suspension or hide the address, and online-only businesses are ineligible [S11].
- **Service-area video:** must show the business address, tools or a branded vehicle, and proof of management (permit, invoice or utility bill) [S12].
- **Sept 2026:** an undocumented storefront photo verification option was spotted [S13].
- **2026:** Google texts or calls the verified number and applies the answers automatically [S14][S15].
- **Advanced Verification** (Ads and LSA) applies only to **garage door (US)** and **locksmith (US/Canada)** [S16]. LSA background-checks technicians for locksmith, plumbing, HVAC, garage door and electrical [S17].
- **Standing rules:**
  - A service area may extend no more than about 2 hours' drive.
  - No unstaffed virtual offices.
  - One profile per business.
  - Lead-generation agents are ineligible [S18][S19].

## 3. Policy changes and review enforcement

- **Profile rules:**
  - Apr: major name or category changes are rejected, and a new business must not rename the previous occupant's profile to inherit its reviews [S20].
  - Aug: bilingual names were banned [S21].
  - Sept: unrejected user edits may publish after 4 days [S22], and Posts with unverified phone numbers get removed [S23].
- **Reviews:**
  - Feb: the policy bans "unusual volumes or patterns" of reviews and incentives [S24]. Mass review removals followed [S25].
  - Apr 17: bans staff review quotas and asking for reviews that name staff [S26][S27].
  - Google removed 292M reviews and 13M fake profiles in 2025 [S7].
- **Review penalties:**
  - Paused reviews, unpublished reviews, and consumer warning banners [S28].
  - Repeat violations double the restriction [S29].
  - "Spike in spam reviews" pauses appeared in Sept 2026 [S30].
  - Maps asks users whether a business rewards reviews [S31].
- **Enforcement:**
  - On May 11, 2026, the DOJ (for the FTC) and the Illinois AG sued **Chicago-based** Premium Home Service.
  - The suit alleges 15,000+ fake Google profiles with keyword-plus-city names (plumbing, heating and cooling, electrical, garage door repair), borrowed addresses and fake reviews, with jobs passed to other contractors [S32][S33][S34].
  - FTC review-rule warning letters went to property managers and personal-injury firms [S35].

## 5. Local Services Ads

- **Badge:** "Google Verified" replaced Guaranteed/Screened (Oct 20, 2025) [S45].
- **Requirements, effective Jul 31, 2026 [S46][S47]:**
  - No raising prices on arrival.
  - No on-site review asks.
  - **Leads must be fulfilled by your own vetted technicians** [S48].
- **Migration into Google Ads as Performance Max:**
  - Phase 1 (Aug 2026) covers plumbing, HVAC, electrical, appliance repair, cleaning, lawn care, roofing, pest control and moving.
  - Service-area businesses follow in late 2026 [S49][S50].
- **Aug 21:**
  - Pre-badge ads let advertisers get leads before verification finishes, **except garage door, healthcare and locksmith**.
  - New beauty, auto and dining verticals have no badge [S51].
- **Pricing:** from **Oct 1, 2026**, missed business-hours calls where the caller waits more than 20 seconds are billable [S52].
- **Lead costs (Tier C):** up 11–69% year over year; appliance repair went from $20 to $34 [S53].
- **Chicago:** no Chicago-specific change was found (unconfirmed) [S17].

## Implications for picking niches

1. **Garage door and locksmith** carry the most friction. Enter only with a licensed, staffed, branded operation.
2. **Chicago plumbing, HVAC, electrical and garage door:** use real legal names and staffed locations; keyword-plus-city names and borrowed addresses are litigation targets.
3. **Referral or partner models are the biggest structural risk.** GBP bars lead-generation agents and LSA requires your own technicians. Each profile must belong to the company doing the work.
4. **Mainstream trades** (cleaning, lawn, pest, moving) have lower friction. Staff the phones, because missed calls are now billable.
5. **Mobile and personal services** (trainers, mobile beauty, pet care) can verify through the service-area path. Answer phones well.
6. **Reviews:** ask every customer neutrally, with no quotas, staff-name prompts or incentives.
7. **Acquisitions:** never rename an acquired profile into a different business [S20].
8. With directions rising and calls falling, storefront or hybrid models may hold up better (inference from [S44]).
