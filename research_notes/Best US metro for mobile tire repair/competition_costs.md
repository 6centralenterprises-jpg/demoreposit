# Competition Intensity and Operating Costs for a Mobile Tire Repair/Replacement Business: Sunbelt Metros vs. Chicago/Detroit (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-12. Each stat below carries its reference date where the source gives one. Coverage is uneven. Wage data is strong. Competitor footprints, Google Maps saturation, commercial auto insurance by state, and current gas prices are weak or missing (see Gaps).

## 1. Competition: which national/regional mobile tire players serve which metros, how saturated is local search, and where are the gaps?

### Takeaway
No public source gives a clean national player-by-metro coverage matrix. What is documented points to Atlanta, Chicago, Las Vegas, Charlotte and Orlando as places where branded or retailer-backed mobile tire installers already operate. Discount Tire mobile also runs in parts of Texas. Retail mobile install is aimed at scheduled replacement for passenger cars. Semi-truck/fleet roadside tire work is a separate, fragmented, hourly-billed market, often served through county or municipal bid contracts.

### Cited Findings
- **Discount Tire mobile installation (as of July 9, 2024).** Offered "in 11 states." Fleet van services ran in 14 states at more than 30 locations. Recent launches were Minneapolis and Midland, TX, with Chicago planned for August 2024. The article does not name the 11 states. — [Modern Tire Dealer, Jul 9 2024](https://www.moderntiredealer.com/retail/article/55093631/discount-tire-expands-mobile-service); Chicago launch confirmed in [Fleet Maintenance](https://fleetmaintenance.com/equipment/brakes-tire-and-wheel/article/55125976/discount-tire-discount-tire-brings-mobile-service-to-chicago)
- **Tread Connection (mobile tire franchise).** Founded 2016 in Charlotte, NC. A 2020 report counted 23 franchisees across the southern U.S. A 2025 franchise listing gives 33 units, but the count's date was not confirmed. In 2021 it formed the "Treadsy Mobile Solutions" partnership with Tirebuyer, which folded franchisees into preferred Tirebuyer.com territories. No current city list was found. — [Tyrepress, Aug 2021](https://tyrepress.com/2021/08/tirebuyer-and-tread-connection-partner-to-create-treadsy-mobile-solutions); [Tire Review](https://www.tirereview.com/?p=184025); [Sharpsheets franchise list 2025](https://sharpsheets.io/blog/best-tire-franchises); [WCCB Charlotte](https://www.wccbcharlotte.com/?p=835109)
- **Tread Connection franchise cost.** Initial investment is $109,500–$186,000 in one comparison and $164,200–$203,400 with a 6% royalty in another. The two sources conflict. — [Sharpsheets](https://sharpsheets.io/blog/best-tire-franchises) and search-summary sources
- **Spiffy Mobile Tire.** Listed as a Tire Rack mobile installer in Las Vegas (ZIPs 89166 and 89158). It is limited to wheels of 28 inches or less, factory-size tires only, with no single-tire orders. — [Tire Rack installers, Las Vegas 89166](https://www.tirerack.com/installers/nv/las-vegas/89166). A Spiffy tire technician job posting dated Feb 13, 2025 places van-based operations in Mableton, GA (Atlanta metro). — [Spiffy job posting](https://jobs.tribecavp.com/companies/get-spiffy/jobs/45965184-tire-technician)
- **Tire Rack Mobile Tire Installation.** Advertises Atlanta, Alpharetta, Marietta, Smyrna and Roswell, plus a Baltimore/Philadelphia corridor. — [Tire Rack mobile install, ZIP 30340](https://www.tirerack.com/mobile-tire-installation?zip=30340); [ZIP 19720](https://www.tirerack.com/mobile-tire-installation?zip=19720)
- **Mavis.** Its site navigation has a "Mobile Tire Service" page and it claims 2,100+ locations, including the Midas acquisition in June 2025. No mobile-service city list was found. — [Mavis tire services](https://mavis.com/tire-services)
- **Independent mobile operators.** Example: RoadrunnersTires mobile tire service covers Orlando, Kissimmee, Davenport and Clermont (Polk/Orange/Lake/Osceola counties). — [Google Business listing](https://business.google.com/v/roadrunnerstires-mobile-tire-services/05174241994073449145/1fb6/_)
- **TireVan.** Launched mobile tire installation in the Philadelphia region, outside the target set. This shows VC-style entrants are going after northern metros as well. — [FenderBender](https://fenderbender.com/running-a-shop/finance/article/33033289/tirevan-expands-mobile-tire-service-offerings-into-philly-region)
- **Commercial/semi-truck tire road service.** Procurement shows public fleets buying it under contract:
  - Lee County, FL's 2025–26 schedule bills emergency tire service per hour, with a 1-hour minimum in regular hours and a 2-hour minimum after hours. — [Lee County FL rate sheet](https://www.leegov.com/procurement/Project%20Documents/PB250057CFC%20-%20Tires,%20Tubes%20and%20Services/BOULEVARD%20TIRE%20SERVICE%20RATES%2025%2026.pdf)
  - Dallas County ran a "Mobile Tire Repair and Services" bid in 2022. — [Dallas County bid tab](https://www.dallascounty.org/Assets/uploads/docs/purchasing/bidtabs/2022/Copy%20of%20Copy%20of%20Mobile%20Tire%20Repair%20and%20Services%20Attachment%20A%20Cost%20Sheet%20(003)%2011-11-21.pdf)
  - Huntsville, TX did the same in 2024. — [Huntsville TX bid tab](https://www.huntsvilletx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/23532/Bid-Tab---NO-24-42-Tire-Repair-Services)

### Inferences
- Retail mobile install from Discount Tire, Tire Rack partners and Spiffy is visibly present in Atlanta, Las Vegas and Chicago. That makes Atlanta and Chicago likely the most contested for scheduled passenger-tire installs among the metros checked.
- Discount Tire is headquartered in Scottsdale, AZ and is dense in Texas and Arizona, so its mobile program plausibly reaches Phoenix and Texas metros. This is not confirmed, because the article does not name the 11 states.
- Big retail mobile programs are scheduled, daytime and passenger-car focused. Spiffy, for example, takes no single-tire orders and only factory sizes. That leaves room for emergency or 24/7 flat repair, single-tire roadside replacement, and semi/fleet tire calls. Public-sector bids show fleets will buy contracted hourly mobile tire service. Truck-heavy freight metros (DFW, Houston, Atlanta, Phoenix, Las Vegas I-15) are the logical places to pursue that.
- Charlotte is Tread Connection's home market, so expect at least one franchise there and probable presence in other Southeast metros.

### Gaps
- No primary source was found for current service areas of Tread Connection/Treadsy, Mobile Tire Pros, "Tirewise"/Tire Agent, Goodyear mobile install, or Mavis mobile. A search for "Tirewise" returned nothing relevant, and the company name may be wrong.
- Google Maps saturation (count of "mobile tire repair near me" listings per metro) was not measured. It needs Maps scraping or a local-SEO tool (e.g., Semrush/OpenRush local SERP) per metro.
- No metro-level evidence was gathered on 24/7 coverage gaps. The gap inference above comes from service restrictions, not measured demand.

## 2. Pricing: mobile flat repair, mobile install, roadside tire change, commercial truck tire calls

### Takeaway
Consumer mobile flat repair is commonly cited at roughly $25–$100 per repair plus a $25–$150 call fee, with on-site replacement at $100–$300+ per tire. The consumer sources are low quality. Commercial and semi tire road service is billed hourly at about $65–$150/hr plus call-out, mileage and minimums in documented bids. Heavy-truck roadside tire calls run $250–$1,200+ per event per a 2026 industry guide.

### Cited Findings
- **Consumer flat repair.** Plug about $25–$75, patch about $35–$100, service-call fee about $25–$50+. Another source gives an average call/tow near $60 and up to $150. Mobile runs about $10–$50 above shop pricing (2025 guide). These come from low-quality SEO pages and should be treated as indicative only. — [mobile tire repair cost guide](https://matrix.mtrx.poff.ee/post/how-much-does-mobile-tire-repair-cost); [fix-a-flat cost 2026](https://new.risingsunartscentre.org/news/fix-a-flat-cost-what-consumers-typically-pay-in-the-us-2026.html); [mobile flat repair cost](https://a.lemon.com.uy/post/mobile-flat-tire-repair-cost)
- **Commercial/municipal bids.**
  - Huntsville, TX 2024 bid tab: $65/hr in-county plus $2.50/mile, with other bidders at $125/hr and $145/hr, and an after-hours per-call-out fee. — [Huntsville TX bid tab NO 24-42](https://www.huntsvilletx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/23532/Bid-Tab---NO-24-42-Tire-Repair-Services)
  - Dallas County 2022 bid: $50 service call per visit per location, and $25 per repair and per mount/dismount for commercial trucks. — [Dallas County 2022 cost sheet](https://www.dallascounty.org/Assets/uploads/docs/purchasing/bidtabs/2022/Copy%20of%20Copy%20of%20Mobile%20Tire%20Repair%20and%20Services%20Attachment%20A%20Cost%20Sheet%20(003)%2011-11-21.pdf)
  - Lee County, FL 2025–26: hourly emergency tire service with a 1-hr minimum (regular hours) and 2-hr minimum (after hours). The dollar rate was not visible in the extract. — [Lee County FL](https://www.leegov.com/procurement/Project%20Documents/PB250057CFC%20-%20Tires,%20Tubes%20and%20Services/BOULEVARD%20TIRE%20SERVICE%20RATES%2025%2026.pdf)
- **Heavy-truck roadside tire service.** $250–$1,200+ per event, more if a tire is replaced. The structure is call-out fee + hourly labor + mileage/deadhead + minimum hours (2026). — [LogRock, 18-wheeler roadside costs 2026](https://www.logrock.com/?p=5741)

### Inferences
- Truck and fleet calls generate several times the revenue of a consumer plug. Government bids show buyers pressing hourly rates down to $65–$145/hr. Private over-the-road emergency calls (after-hours, minimum hours, mileage) are likely the higher-margin segment.

### Gaps
- No metro-specific price survey was found. Pricing was not compared across DFW vs. Atlanta vs. Phoenix and so on. A mystery-shop or quote collection per metro would be required.
- No authoritative published price lists were found from national mobile players (Discount Tire mobile fee, Spiffy fee, Tread Connection fee).

## 3. Costs: technician wages, insurance, taxes, fuel, space

### Takeaway
Tire-tech wages vary only about 15% across the metros. Florida metros are cheapest (Orlando median $33,580) and Phoenix/Chicago/Las Vegas the most expensive (about $38,200–$38,700), per BLS OEWS May 2025. Texas metros sit in the middle and have the deepest labor pools: DFW has about 3,690 and Houston about 3,110 tire repairers. Florida and Nevada have the highest personal-auto insurance, and Texas has among the cheapest gas (September 2026). Industrial rent data conflicts heavily between sources.

### Inferences
- **Labor.** Florida metros (Orlando, Jacksonville, Tampa) have the lowest tire-tech pay, about $1.50–$2.50/hr under Phoenix, Las Vegas and Chicago. The Texas metros pair middling pay with the largest hiring pools, and DFW + Houston account for about 6,800 workers. Las Vegas has the thinnest pool (490), which could make hiring harder.
- **Insurance.** Personal-auto proxies point to Florida as the highest-cost state for vehicle insurance among those checked, with Texas and Georgia mid-range. Commercial van quotes will differ and need broker quotes.
- **Income tax (general knowledge, not verified in this session's sources).** Texas, Florida, Nevada and Tennessee levy no personal income tax on wages. Texas instead has a franchise (margin) tax with a no-tax-due revenue threshold, Nevada a Commerce Tax for large businesses, and Tennessee franchise and excise taxes. Verify before relying on this.
- **Fuel.** Fuel costs favor Texas metros as of September 2026.
- **Space.** Small shops for a van-based operator are small-bay or flex space, which costs more per SF than big-box averages. Atlanta's small-bay range of $12–$16 illustrates this. A mobile operator may need only van parking plus tire storage, making rent a minor line item relative to labor, insurance and fuel.

### Gaps
- Commercial auto insurance premiums by state for a tire-service van (TX, GA, AZ, FL, NC, TN, NV) were not found and need broker or carrier quotes.
- Current (October 2026) AAA state gas averages for GA, FL, NC, TN, IL and MI were not found. Diesel prices were not captured.
- Combined sales tax rates by metro city were not collected, apart from Tennessee's state average.
- Las Vegas, Charlotte, Nashville, Jacksonville, Tampa, San Antonio, Austin, Chicago and Detroit industrial rents were not found. Phoenix data is contradictory.
- Direct BLS pages could not be fetched. Wage figures rely on Wagedex's restatement of BLS May 2025.

## 4. Regulatory friction: tire disposal, used-tire sales, towing/roadside licensing

### Takeaway
Texas, Florida, Georgia and North Carolina all regulate hauling scrap/used tires. Georgia is the heaviest: a per-location carrier permit, financial assurance, decals, manifests and no storage. Texas requires TCEQ transporter registration, manifests, 3-year records and annual reports. Florida requires DEP collector registration above 25 tires, with fees of $50 or less per vehicle. North Carolina's registration is free and lasts 5 years, and it exempts tire retailers hauling their own replacement tires.

### Cited Findings
**Texas (TCEQ, 30 TAC 328 Subchapter F)**
- Transporters of used or scrap tires must register with TCEQ before collecting, deliver only to authorized facilities, submit annual reports and must not stockpile.
- A manifest system and 3-year record retention are required.
- Generators hauling their own tires between their own sites or to an authorized facility are exempt. A mobile installer hauling customers' tires should verify whether it counts as the generator.
- Application: transporter form plus Core Data Form TCEQ-10400.
- A 2009 bill analysis (SB 459) allowed TCEQ to require financial assurance; current applicability is unconfirmed.
- Sources: [TCEQ transporter requirements](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/waste_permits/tires/tires-transport); [TCEQ RG-510](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/downloads/permitting/waste-permits/publications/rg-510.pdf/view); [TCEQ regulated activities](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/waste_permits/tires/tires-regulated-activities); [SB 459 analysis](https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/83R/analysis/pdf/SB00459H.pdf)

**Florida (DEP, s. 403.717 F.S., Ch. 62-711 F.A.C.)**
- Anyone moving more than 25 waste tires on Florida roads must register as a waste tire collector.
- Fees are capped by statute at $50 per vehicle per year. Older notices list $35 decals.
- Renewals are due March 1. The DEP page showed decals expiring April 1, 2025, so the current cycle needs confirming.
- Businesses using a collector for more than 25 tires/month per location must keep transport records.
- Mobile waste-tire processing equipment needs a General Permit per unit.
- Sources: [Fla. Stat. 403.717](https://m.flsenate.gov/Statutes/403.717); [Florida DEP](https://floridadep.gov/taxonomy/term/534?page=40)

**Georgia (EPD)**
- Hauling non-new tires requires a tire carrier permit and a decal on each vehicle.
- Financial assurance is required per business location.
- Carriers may not store scrap tires.
- Tires go only to EPD-approved facilities, with the manifest returned to the generator within 30 days.
- Each business location needs a separate application.
- A retailer moving its own used tires with proof of purchase is exempt.
- Sources: [GA EPD tire carrier responsibilities](https://epd.georgia.gov/document/document/tire-carrier-responsibilities/download); [GA EPD carrier permit application](https://epd.georgia.gov/sites/epd.georgia.gov/files/related_files/site_page/Tire%20Carrier%20Permit%20Application.docx); [GA EPD generator responsibilities](https://epd.georgia.gov/document/document/scrap-tire-generator-responsibilities/download)

**North Carolina (DEQ, G.S. 130A-309.59)**
- Scrap tire hauler registration is required before hauling. It is free, must be filed at least 30 days ahead, and is valid 5 years.
- Licensed tire retailers hauling tires from their own replacement sales are exempt.
- Sources: [G.S. 130A-309.59](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_130A/GS_130A-309.59.html); [NC DEQ hauler application](https://www.deq.nc.gov/documents/files/scraptirehaulerapplication2018/open)

### Inferences
- **Lowest disposal-compliance friction (among states checked):** NC (free and long-lived, with a retailer exemption) and FL (light fee).
- **Moderate:** TX (registration, manifests, reporting).
- **Highest:** GA (financial assurance, per-location permits).
- Regardless of state, a mobile tire business must budget per-tire disposal fees at an authorized facility. Those fees were not researched here.

### Gaps
- Arizona (ADEQ), Tennessee (TDEC), Nevada (NDEP), Illinois (IEPA) and Michigan (EGLE) scrap tire hauler rules were not found in this session.
- Used-tire resale rules (tread-depth minimums, state bans on selling unsafe used tires) were not researched.
- Towing/roadside-assistance licensing was not researched; for example, Texas TDLR towing licenses apply only if towing is offered. No source confirmed whether pure mobile tire service triggers any towing license in these states.
- Per-tire state fees on new tire sales (e.g., Texas has none at retail since 1997, while Florida, Georgia, Tennessee and others charge per-tire fees) were not verified in sources this session.
