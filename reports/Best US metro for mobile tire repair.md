# Launch TJ's Tire Rescue from Grand Prairie

**Recommendation (judgement, built on cited facts):** If the 6 Central Enterprises portfolio started one mobile tire repair business in a perfect world, it should be **TJ's Tire Rescue**, based in **Dallas-Fort Worth**, with a small drive-in bay at **2701–2749 S Great Southwest Pkwy, Grand Prairie, TX** as the staging depot. DFW leads on the factors this research could verify. It added about **123,600 residents in 2025**, second only to Houston. It has the largest US industrial construction pipeline, which means dense truck and delivery-van traffic. It also has the deepest tire-technician labor pool of any metro checked, Texas gas is among the cheapest in the country, and Sunbelt weather allows work all year. The Great Southwest district sits between I-30, SH-360, I-20 and SH-161, roughly halfway between Dallas and Fort Worth, so one depot can cover both cores. The brand name ties the new business to TJ's Nationwide Roadside, and our checks found no conflicting business or live .com for it. **Houston** is the close runner-up, with a depot at 7125 W Tidwell Rd. **Atlanta** comes third, with a depot at 975 Camp Fulton Way SW, Suite B. **Every listing below is a snapshot from public listing sites, and some are dated 2025 or spring 2026.** Small bays lease quickly, so treat each address as a lead to confirm with the broker, not as space you can rent today. The biggest open risk is local permitting: Grand Prairie requires a Specific Use Permit for auto repair in Light Industrial zones, and the depot needs a zoning check before anyone signs a lease.

## DFW wins on growth, freight and labor depth

Population growth is the strongest demand signal the research could document. In Census Vintage 2025, Houston (about **126,700**) and DFW (about **123,600**) each added roughly twice as many people as Atlanta (**61,953**), and Phoenix (**59,065**) came next ([RealPage](https://www.realpage.com/analytics/population-growth-markets-2025/); [ABC15](https://www.abc15.com/news/business/new-census-data-only-two-major-u-s-metros-outpaced-the-valleys-growth-rate-in-2025)). The Census Bureau reports that this decade's growth is concentrated on metro outer edges ([Census Bureau](https://census.gov/library/stories/2026/05/major-city-outer-edge-growth.html)). Our judgement is that this favors mobile service: exurban drivers live farther from fixed tire shops, and new construction zones put more nails and screws on the road.

DFW pulls ahead of Houston on freight. It has about **1.0 billion sq ft of industrial space**, the largest US construction pipeline in mid-2025 (28–29M sq ft), and nearly **25M sq ft of net absorption in 2025** ([CBRE](https://www.cbre.com/press-releases/3pls-take-the-lead-in-2025s-top-100-industrial-leases); [MMCG](https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/the-top-five-u-s-industrial-markets-in-2025-an-investor-s-deep-dive)). That matters because fleet and truck calls bring in several times the revenue of a consumer tire plug. Public bids show how commercial tire road service is priced. Huntsville, TX received bids of **$65–$145/hr plus $2.50/mile** ([Huntsville TX bid tab](https://www.huntsvilletx.gov/DocumentCenter/View/23532/Bid-Tab---NO-24-42-Tire-Repair-Services)). Dallas County ran its own mobile tire repair bid at **$50 per service call** ([Dallas County](https://www.dallascounty.org/Assets/uploads/docs/purchasing/bidtabs/2022/Copy%20of%20Copy%20of%20Mobile%20Tire%20Repair%20and%20Services%20Attachment%20A%20Cost%20Sheet%20(003)%2011-11-21.pdf)). A 2026 industry guide puts heavy-truck roadside tire calls at **$250–$1,200+ per event** ([LogRock](https://www.logrock.com/?p=5741)).

Operating costs are also favorable. DFW has **3,690 tire repairers** at a **$36,600 median**, the largest pool of any metro checked. Phoenix pays more ($38,600) and has a little over half as many workers ([Wagedex/BLS May 2025](https://wagedex.com/jobs/tire-repairers-and-changers)). Texas tied for the **lowest state gas average at $3.96/gal** in September 2026, compared with a national average of $4.48 ([Voronoi](https://www.voronoiapp.com/economy/Mapped-Gas-Prices-by-US-State-in-2026-9128)). Demand from missing spares is national: about **one-third of new vehicles lack a spare tire** ([Journal-News](https://www.journal-news.com/classifieds/cars/spare-nearly-one-third-new-vehicles-are-missing-spare-tire/8C67XkO99MOIdNJMk5lYPI/)), and AAA handled more than **30 million roadside calls in 2025**, with blown tires among the most common ([AAA MWG](https://mwg.aaa.com/news/press-release/may-5-2026)). Our judgement is that DFW's heat raises blowout volume in summer without the snow downtime northern metros face.

The case against DFW deserves a fair hearing. Houston grew slightly faster, and its flex vacancy of **11.8% (Q1 2026)** gives tenants more leverage on rent ([Partners Q1 2026](https://partnersrealestate.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Q126_Hou_Industrial_QuarterlyReport.pdf)). The California metros and New York have far worse roads, with **53–64% of major roads rated poor**, but that TRIP data is several years old ([Equipment World](https://www.equipmentworld.com/business/article/14948004/the-top-20-urban-roads-in-poor-condition)), and those markets carry higher costs and seasonal swings. Discount Tire offers mobile installation in Texas, including Midland ([Modern Tire Dealer](https://www.moderntiredealer.com/retail/article/55093631/discount-tire-expands-mobile-service)). Retail mobile programs like Spiffy's, however, are scheduled, daytime and passenger-car only, and Spiffy takes **no single-tire orders** ([Tire Rack](https://www.tirerack.com/installers/nv/las-vegas/89166)). Our judgement is that emergency flat repair, single-tire roadside replacement, after-hours calls and fleet work are under-served. Those services fit naturally with TJ's Nationwide Roadside.

## Permitting, scrap tires and the brand name are the real risks

**Zoning.** Grand Prairie hearing agendas show **Specific Use Permit requests for "Major Auto Repair" on Light Industrial land**, including one in 2026 ([GP PD 208](https://www.gptx.org/files/sharedassets/public/departments/planning/documents/planneddevelopmentdistricts/PD_208.pdf); [GP P&Z](https://grandprairietx.new.swagit.com/videos/393506)). A depot used only to park vans, stock parts and dispatch technicians may be classified differently from a repair shop, but the city has to make that call. **Fire code.** Under the model fire code, **all indoor tire storage** needs an operational permit, scrap tire storage above **2,500 cu ft** needs a permit, and tire piles over **6' high covering more than 500 SF** count as high-piled storage ([Vancouver VFD citing IFC](https://cityofvancouver.us/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Fillable-VFD-Scrap-Tire-Storage-Permit-Application-OPS.pdf)). Houston uses the same 2,500 cu ft threshold ([Houston Permitting Center](https://www.houstonpermittingcenter.org/media/7556/download)). Our judgement is that a small rack of new tires stays below the high-piled triggers, but the business should still expect to need an indoor-storage permit.

**Scrap tires.** In Texas, anyone transporting used or scrap tires must **register with TCEQ before collecting any**, deliver only to authorized facilities, use manifests, keep records for **3 years**, file annual reports, and must not stockpile tires ([TCEQ](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/permitting/waste_permits/tires/tires-transport); [RG-510](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/downloads/permitting/waste-permits/publications/rg-510.pdf/view)). Generators hauling their own tires are exempt. Whether a mobile installer that removes a customer's tire counts as the "generator" is an **open question** that TCEQ should answer in writing. Per-tire disposal fees were not researched. **Insurance.** No state-level commercial van premiums were found. The only national benchmark is about **$84–$93/month** for standard commercial vehicles, and location can move rates by up to 33% ([MoneyGeek](https://www.moneygeek.com/insurance/business/commercial-auto/cost)). Real broker quotes are required. Texas personal-auto proxies sit mid-range, below Florida ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/insurance/how-much-is-car-insurance?lv=true)).

**Brand.** We ran DNS lookups on 2026-10-12. **tjstirerescue.com did not resolve**, and neither did tjsmobiletire.com or centraltirerescue.com. tirerescue.com, flatpatrol.com and treadrunner.com did resolve, so they appear to be taken. A domain that does not resolve is not proof that it is available. Web search found no business called "TJ's Tire Rescue." The similar names it did find, such as TJ's Tire & Auto in NJ and WI, are not mobile and not in Texas ([SimpleTire](https://simpletire.com/tire-shops/shop/tjs-tire-auto-repair-llc/78855)). Names to avoid: **TIRE TAXI**, a filed mark covering tire and roadside repair ([Justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/owners/mobile-fleet-medic-llc-5093652)); **TIRE RUNNER**, a registered mark ([Justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/862/29/tire-86229269.html)); and names built on "Roll," because of Goodyear's "Roll by Goodyear" ([The Drive](https://thedrive.com/news/24436/goodyear-starts-streamlined-tire-installation-service-in-maryland)). Our judgement is to choose **TJ's Tire Rescue** first, with **TJ's Mobile Tire** as the backup. The TJ's name lets the new business share reviews and cross-sell with TJ's Nationwide Roadside. Its weakness is that "Tire Rescue" is descriptive, which makes the trademark harder to protect.

## Conclusion

The best metro does not depend on a single factor. DFW wins because growth, freight volume, labor supply and fuel costs all point the same way, while Houston wins on growth alone and California wins only on road condition. The bigger gap in the market is what large retail mobile programs leave unserved: emergency, single-tire and fleet calls. Pairing a new tire business with an existing roadside brand fits that gap better than competing for scheduled tire installs.

None of this is final until four checks clear: the unit is actually available, Grand Prairie confirms the depot's use is allowed, TCEQ confirms how disposal works, and a USPTO search comes back clean. Each can be done within about two weeks for little cost. If any one fails, the Houston month-to-month suite is the cheapest way to start operating while the DFW issue is resolved.
