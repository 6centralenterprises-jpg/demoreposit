# Niche hunting: finding wide-open local niches

Read this on Saturdays (the "Emerging & discovery" cluster), and whenever Terell
asks for new niches. It turns one source video into a repeatable method, keeps
the parts that hold up, and drops the parts that would get a profile suspended.

## Source video: what it actually teaches

"All Niches That Works With Drop Servicing GOD COURSE", from the channel
Godfather Of Drop servicing (uploaded 2020-02-28, 24:39). It's a bonus lesson
from a paid course: a spoken list of niches, plus the reasoning behind them.

The model it teaches ("drop servicing", also called rank-and-rent):
1. Pick a niche, search "[niche] + city", and confirm the Google map pack is weak.
2. Rank a Google Business Profile and website for it.
3. Find local providers (often on Craigslist), forward the calls to them, and keep
   a referral fee. If online payments don't convert, switch to selling the leads.

The niches it names: passenger van and yacht rental, escape rooms, haunted houses,
golf courses, makeup artists, "celebrity" versions (personal trainer, nutritionist,
makeup artist, publicist, realtor, nanny agency, tours), comedians, music marketing,
mobile DJ, translation services, celebrity impersonators, flyboarding, parasailing,
kite-surfing lessons, call centers, barber and cosmetology schools, carports, room
additions, pool deck resurfacing, patio covers, art and music consultants,
locticians, airports, passport offices, credit unions and private equity firms.

## What we keep (the principles that are sound)

1. **Hunt where SEOs don't look.** The presenter's best ideas are niches
   marketers ignore: leisure and experiences, creative-industry services,
   training schools, and high-ticket home add-ons. Their map packs stay
   thin because nobody has built strong profiles there.
2. **High-ticket modifiers change the buyer.** "Room additions" (~$9k per job,
   in the presenter's words) or a premium positioning attracts a different
   customer than the generic search. Score job value via CPC as well as volume.
3. **Search intent beats interruption.** People who search are already looking
   to buy; ads that interrupt them are not. Google search is the channel.
4. **Research before spending.** Check volume and map-pack strength before
   building anything. The scout's scoring is exactly this step.
5. **Repeat customers matter.** Fun and lifestyle services (lessons, rentals,
   events) can sell memberships and repeat bookings.
6. **Build capacity, not a single point of failure.** Line up several real
   operators in a city so one being booked solid doesn't drop the customer.

## What we drop, and the legitimate version

| Video tactic | Why it's out | What 6 Central does instead |
|---|---|---|
| Rank a profile for a business you don't run, then forward the calls | Google bars lead-generation businesses from holding profiles. This is the pattern behind suspension waves, and it hurts the real providers too. | **Partner & manage:** the operator owns the profile and 6 Central manages it with Manager access. Or 6 Central runs the service itself. |
| Listings like "airport", "passport office", "credit union", "private equity firm" | That isn't a business you can be. These listings would impersonate a category or an institution. | Skip. |
| "Celebrity [service]" keywords without celebrity clients | This implies a clientele you don't have, which is a misleading claim. | Use premium positioning that's true ("luxury", "concierge", "white-glove") when the service really delivers it. |
| Ranking for "who does marketing for Apple" so clients assume you do | It works only through a false impression. | Publish real case studies under your own name. |
| Craigslist operators handing over their portfolio photos for your ads | Their work, shown under your brand, misattributes it. | Show each operator's work under their own business name, with permission. |

The video's results ("instantly rank", "no competition in every city", "$30k
client", "$9,000 per deal") are the presenter's claims. Nothing here has
verified them. Check every niche with live data instead.

## The method, as the scout runs it

1. **Generate candidates** by applying the principles above to a theme:
   - ignored categories
   - high-ticket add-ons to an existing service
   - premium versions of a service
   - repeat-booking experiences
2. **Filter for 6 Central fit:**
   - a real operator can do it from a home base or a shop
   - licensing is known (`licensed: true` in config if a license is needed)
   - it isn't an institution or a regulated professional body
3. **Semrush batch:** volume, CPC and keyword difficulty (KD). Keep a keyword if:
   - volume is at least 1,000 a month, or
   - CPC is at least $8 and volume is at least 300 a month, or
   - KD is at most 15 and volume is at least 500 a month.
4. **Map-pack check in Chicago first:** a median under 50 reviews is open; under
   100 is a contender.
5. **Score and play:** use the normal scout scoring. A licensed niche becomes
   Partner & manage, never Own it.
6. **Add the survivors to a config cluster** so the daily rotation tracks them.
